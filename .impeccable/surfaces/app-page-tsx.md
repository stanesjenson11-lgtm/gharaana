# Surface brief: home hero (app/page.tsx)

## Scope and visitor mode

Persuade, with an Experience core. One route: a scroll-driven, real-time 3D tour for Gharaana, about nine viewports of pinned scroll, ending on a booking close.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

- Audience: premium buyers and investors (confirmed).
- Job: decide whether Gharaana is worth contacting; search or book a viewing.
- Primary action in the first viewport: property search (Buy / Rent / Invest, place, Search homes). Secondary: Book a viewing in the nav and at the close.
- Proof: one continuous camera through one model. Above the clouds, the Gharaana tower among a skyline, through one window into the same flat, up through the ceiling to that flat's floor plan, up to the neighbourhood map with the tower pinned, then the city at golden hour.
- Constraints: realistic look (user direction), no night or outdoor lights, illustrative model labelled in the footer, CC0 Poly Haven assets hotlinked for now, reduced-motion path cuts between stops.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one continuous shot of one building. Nothing cuts: the skyline, the tower, the window, the room, its plan and its neighbourhood are the same model seen by one camera. It refuses the category default of a photo hero with a search card over it, and refuses stitched stock photos of different buildings.

OWN-WORLD: Real-time archviz realism. Physical sky with lit cumulus, low morning mist, a white tower whose wavy balcony slabs are the logo's contour lines, brand-yellow lines on its first two slab edges, glass that reflects the actual city, a herringbone-parquet flat with Poly Haven furniture, leaf-card trees, a varied limestone/sandstone/brick/glass city. Brand blue and yellow live in the UI chrome and the flat's cushions and print. Neutral tone mapping, soft shadows, ambient occlusion, faint grain.

STORY: The visitor understands in seconds that this is a place to enter, believes Gharaana shows homes with care because the tour does, and searches or books.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Morning above the mist; the Gharaana tower centre, skyline towers rising around it, cumulus overhead, a flock of birds. Headline "Step inside your next address." centred at about 6rem, scroll prompt, and the white pill search bar below it. Nav top, stop rail right.

FORM: Brief-pinned scroll-through journey from the user's reel, rebuilt as one real-time 3D world after user feedback (no concept roll, no seed key).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
